Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Effective date: July 20, 2026

DartsCool is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal data.

Data collection

DartsCool does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Game settings, language and voice preferences, and match information are stored locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to us. You can remove it by deleting the app.

Bluetooth dartboards

If you choose to connect a compatible Bluetooth dartboard, DartsCool uses Bluetooth only to discover the board and receive throw data. Bluetooth information and scores are processed locally and are not sent to us.

Voice and audio

Score callouts use the device's speech-synthesis features. DartsCool does not access the microphone or record audio.

Children's privacy

Because DartsCool does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app's features change. Questions can be sent to bbc6bae9@gmail.com.

DartsCool 无需注册账号即可使用，并以不收集个人数据为设计原则。

数据收集

DartsCool 不收集、出售、共享或传输个人信息。应用不包含广告、分析、跟踪或第三方数据收集 SDK。

设备本地数据

比赛设置、语言与语音偏好、比赛信息仅保存在你的设备上，不会上传给我们。删除应用即可移除这些本地数据。

蓝牙飞镖盘

当你主动连接兼容的蓝牙飞镖盘时，DartsCool 仅使用蓝牙发现设备并接收投镖数据。蓝牙信息与分数均在本地处理，不会发送给我们。

语音与音频

语音报分使用设备的语音合成功能。DartsCool 不访问麦克风，也不会录制音频。

儿童隐私

由于 DartsCool 不收集个人信息，因此不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新与联系我们

如果应用功能发生变化，我们可能更新本政策。如有问题，请发送邮件至 bbc6bae9@gmail.com。
