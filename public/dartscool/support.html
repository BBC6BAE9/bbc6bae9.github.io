Bluetooth dartboards

DartsCool currently supports compatible DB2 and DB6 Bluetooth dartboards. Turn on the board, allow Bluetooth access, and open the input-device section in DartsCool. Keep the board close to your device while connecting.

Correcting a score

Use Undo during a match to remove an incorrect throw. Match statistics update together with the score.

Your data

DartsCool stores settings and match information locally. It does not require an account or upload this information. Read the privacy policy for details.

连接蓝牙飞镖盘

DartsCool 目前支持兼容的 DB2 与 DB6 蓝牙飞镖盘。请开启飞镖盘、允许蓝牙权限，然后在 DartsCool 的输入设备区域连接。连接时请让飞镖盘靠近设备。

修正错误分数

比赛中可使用“撤销”移除错误投镖，比赛统计会与分数同步更新。

你的数据

DartsCool 仅在本地保存设置和比赛信息，无需账号，也不会上传这些信息。详情请阅读隐私政策。
